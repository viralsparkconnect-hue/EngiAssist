Know the difference

DCR vs Non-DCR

Not sure which one you need? The right choice depends on whether you want a subsidy.

What is DCR?

  • Domestic Content Requirement — cells and modules made in India
  • Required for subsidy-linked schemes such as PM Surya Ghar
  • Best for residential rooftops seeking a subsidy

Choose DCR if…

  • You want the central subsidy on your home system
  • Your project falls under a government scheme
  • You want fully documented, scheme-ready installation

What is Non-DCR?

  • Panels without the domestic content requirement
  • Not eligible for DCR-linked subsidy
  • Often used for commercial and industrial projects

Choose Non-DCR if…

  • You're a business and subsidy isn't the goal
  • You want more panel choices and lower upfront cost
  • You need a faster turnaround
